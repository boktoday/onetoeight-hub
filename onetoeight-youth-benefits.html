one to eight
For young people

What changes when you finish the eight

Eight weeks. Eight essentials. Not a course you complete and forget. A foundation you build the rest of your life on.

The outcome

You become someone who can hold their own life.

Not because someone told you to. Because you proved it to yourself, one week at a time.

01

You can sit still

Three minutes of silence feels normal, not like torture. You can think a full thought without reaching for your phone. You notice things other people miss.

After: You own your attention
02

You trust your body

You sleep better. You cook real food. You move because it feels good, not because you're punishing yourself. Your body is your ally, not your enemy.

After: You have energy for what matters
03

You're actually useful

You can feed yourself, fix things, build things, grow something, and handle money without panic. People ask you for help. You don't Google everything.

After: You're competent, not dependent
04

You control your phone

Not the other way around. You know what it's doing to your attention, your mood, your sleep. You use it on purpose. You can put it down and stay down.

After: You're the boss of your attention
05

You have real people

Not followers. Friends who know your name, who'd pick up at 2am, who tell you the truth. You know how to repair a friendship when it breaks.

After: You're not alone
06

You know things without Wi-Fi

Phone numbers, directions, how to change a tyre, what to do when someone is bleeding. Your knowledge is in you, not in a search bar.

After: You're reliable in a crisis
07

Nature restores you

You know how to use sunlight, soil, and silence to reset. You don't need an app to feel human. You go outside and it actually works.

After: You have a reset button
08

You know what you stand for

Not what your feed says. Not what your friends think. What you actually believe, tested under pressure. Values that hold when life gets expensive.

After: You're unshakeable
"I didn't realise how much noise was in my head until I sat in silence for three minutes. It was the hardest and best thing I did all year."

The shift

Before and after the eight weeks

Before

  • Phone is the first thing you see in the morning
  • You can't name three people who'd help you move
  • You've never cooked a full meal from scratch
  • You feel anxious when you're not entertained
  • You don't know what you believe about anything big
  • You scroll to avoid feeling things

After

  • You sit in silence for 3 minutes before checking your phone
  • You have a pod of people who know your name
  • You can feed yourself, fix things, and grow something
  • You use your phone on purpose, not by default
  • You know your values and you've tested them
  • You feel things and let them pass through
