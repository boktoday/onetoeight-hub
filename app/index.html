Full Description

What is onetoeight?

A practical guide, a weekly meetup, and a complete formation kit — now on your phone. onetoeight walks you through eight essentials that build a life that holds: from the ability to sit in silence, to tested faith.

What's included

  • The full 140-page guide — readable offline
  • 80 original prayers with audio narration (UK male voice)
  • Searchable scripture database with 56 verses and 8 reading plans
  • Audio guide — every chapter narrated
  • Progress tracking through the eight essentials
  • Extra activities for each essential
  • Mini course — 8 bite-sized sessions
  • Leader pack companion (session sheets, challenge cards)
  • Offline AI tools — works without internet
  • Bonus vetted resources with QR codes

Who it's for

  • Young people (ages 15–25)
  • Parents
  • Youth groups & Sunday school classes
  • Anyone looking for depth, skill, and faith

Eight essentials. One journey. No hype. No noise. Just the foundations that hold when life gets real — from the inside out, one through eight.
