EssentialScripture References
1. Silence
Stillness, self-awareness		 Psalm 46:10 — "Be still, and know that I am God."
Psalm 37:7 — "Be still before the Lord and wait patiently for him."
Psalm 131:2 — "I have calmed and quieted my soul, like a weaned child with its mother."
Isaiah 26:3 — "You keep him in perfect peace whose mind is stayed on you."
Lamentations 3:25-26 — "The Lord is good to those who wait for him, to the soul who seeks him. It is good that one should wait quietly."
2. Body
Temple, maintenance, boundaries		 1 Corinthians 6:19-20 — "Your body is a temple of the Holy Spirit... glorify God in your body."
Romans 12:1 — "Present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God."
3 John 1:2 — "I pray that all may go well with you and that you may be in good health."
Proverbs 31:17 — "She dresses herself with strength and makes her arms strong."
Daniel 1:8-16 — Daniel chooses clean food; God honours it.
3. Skills
Work, competence, stewardship		 Colossians 3:23 — "Whatever you do, work heartily, as for the Lord and not for men."
Proverbs 22:29 — "Do you see a man skilful in his work? He will stand before kings."
1 Thessalonians 4:11-12 — "Work with your hands... so that you may walk properly before outsiders."
Proverbs 31:16-18 — "She considers a field and buys it; with the fruit of her hands she plants a vineyard."
Ecclesiastes 9:10 — "Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with all your might."
4. Digital Self-Defence
Privacy, wisdom, guarding gates		 Proverbs 4:23 — "Keep your heart with all vigilance, for from it flow the springs of life."
Matthew 10:16 — "Be wise as serpents and innocent as doves."
1 Peter 5:8 — "Be sober-minded; be watchful. Your adversary the devil prowls around."
Psalm 101:3 — "I will not set before my eyes anything that is worthless."
Ephesians 5:15-16 — "Look carefully then how you walk, not as unwise but as wise, making the best use of the time."
5. Community
Friendship, belonging, burden-bearing		 Proverbs 17:17 — "A friend loves at all times, and a brother is born for adversity."
Ecclesiastes 4:9-10 — "Two are better than one... if one falls, the other lifts him up."
Galatians 6:2 — "Bear one another's burdens, and so fulfil the law of Christ."
Hebrews 10:24-25 — "Consider how to stir up one another to love and good works, not neglecting to meet together."
Acts 2:42-47 — The early church: devotion, fellowship, sharing, gladness.
6. Knowledge
Wisdom, internalised truth		 Psalm 119:11 — "I have stored up your word in my heart, that I might not sin against you."
Proverbs 4:7 — "The beginning of wisdom is this: get wisdom, and whatever you get, get insight."
Colossians 3:16 — "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly."
Deuteronomy 6:6-7 — "These words shall be on your heart. You shall teach them diligently to your children."
Hosea 4:6 — "My people are destroyed for lack of knowledge."
7. Nature
Creation, wonder, grounding		 Psalm 19:1 — "The heavens declare the glory of God; the skies proclaim the work of his hands."
Psalm 95:4-5 — "In his hand are the depths of the earth; the mountain peaks belong to him."
Romans 1:20 — "His invisible attributes... have been clearly perceived ever since the creation of the world."
Genesis 1:31 — "God saw everything that he had made, and behold, it was very good."
Job 12:7-10 — "Ask the beasts, and they will teach you... in his hand is the life of every living thing."
8. Convictions
Tested faith, endurance, standing firm		 James 1:2-4 — "Count it all joy... the testing of your faith produces steadfastness."
Romans 5:3-5 — "Suffering produces endurance, endurance produces character, character produces hope."
1 Peter 1:6-7 — "So that the tested genuineness of your faith... may be found to result in praise."
Ephesians 6:13-14 — "Take up the whole armour of God, that you may be able to withstand... Stand therefore."
Hebrews 12:1-2 — "Let us run with endurance the race set before us, looking to Jesus."
How to Use

Open each session by reading one passage aloud. Let it sit for 30 seconds. Ask: "What does this tell us about [essential]?" Or close by reading the passage as a benediction. For longer sessions, have participants look up the passages themselves and share what they notice.
