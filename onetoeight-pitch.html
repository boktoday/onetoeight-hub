The problem

Community is in freefall. Young people are paying the price.

We gave a generation screens and took away their formation. The effects are not theoretical — they are measurable in anxiety, loneliness, financial fragility, and a quiet erosion of the skills that make a functioning adult.

📱

Connection without contact

The average young person has 600+ online "friends" and fewer than 2 people they could call at 2am. Isolation masquerades as community.

💰

Earning without stewardship

Young people earn more than any generation before them and save less. Financial literacy is not taught in most schools. The skills gap is widening faster than the income gap.

🧠

Stimulation without resilience

An entire generation has been trained for intermittent reward, not sustained effort. When life invoices them — grief, failure, disappointment — there is no load-bearing framework underneath.

🏘️

Access without belonging

Community institutions (churches, clubs, guilds, extended family) have weakened or disappeared. Young people are surrounded by information and starving for initiation into adulthood.

🛠️

Content without competence

Information is abundant. Practical skill — cooking, fixing, building, growing, budgeting — is rare. The gap between online cleverness and offline readiness has become a chasm.

🌪️

Visibility without purpose

Performance culture demands constant output. Young people build brands before they build character. Without deep roots, the storm is permanent.

Local AI · standalone

A formation assistant that lives on the device.

No cloud, no API key, no user data leaving the phone. A quantised 1-billion-parameter language model (~780MB) runs entirely in the browser via WebGPU. The young person downloads it once (cached), and then the AI works offline forever.

🤖

Local LLM in the browser

Uses WebLLM by MLC AI to run a Llama 3.2 1B or Phi-3 model at 4-bit quantisation. ~780MB download, ~2GB RAM at runtime. Runs on Chrome/Edge with WebGPU. No server, no API call, no data exfiltration.

📖

RAG knowledge base

The 8 essentials, prayers, scripture, and practices are embedded into a local knowledge base (~50KB). When the user asks a question, relevant content is retrieved and fed into the model context window. Lightweight keyword scoring works for this content scale.

🙏

Daily prayers from personal goals

The AI can generate a personalised daily prayer or reflection by combining the user stated goals with the language and framework of the essentials. The user types their goal and the AI produces a grounded, honest prayer that references the relevant essential without ever sending that goal to a server.

Architecture · privacy by default

The app lives on the device. Not in the cloud.

Young people should not need to trust another platform with their data to grow. The entire app runs locally using IndexedDB and localStorage — no account, no password, no cloud sync. Progress, reflections, checklists, and conversations stay on the phone. Sharing to a circle happens through apps they already trust.

🏠

Local-first by design

IndexedDB stores challenges, checklists, audio position, wisdom favourites, and reflection journal. Nothing is uploaded to a server unless the user chooses to export. No advertising profile, no behavioural data sale, no surveillance vector.

📤

Share via existing chat apps

Progress cards, completed challenge shares, and weekly summaries use the native Web Share API. A tap sends a beautifully formatted card to WhatsApp, Telegram, Signal, or iMessage — no in-app chat system to build, moderate, or secure. Young people use the apps they already open.

📦

Export / import / backup

One-tap export produces a portable JSON file. Import it on another device or send it to a mentor or parent for accountability. No vendor lock-in. Your growth data belongs to you, not to a startup's database.

Why this matters for investors: Zero cloud infrastructure cost for user data. No GDPR/COPPA compliance burden for storing children's data on our servers. No moderation liability. Viral distribution through native chat sharing. The formation happens on device; the marketing happens through the share button.
