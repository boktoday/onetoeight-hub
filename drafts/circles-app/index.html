How to use onetoeight

What this is onetoeight is an education and awareness program. The 8 essentials are insights and frames for thinking — not a sales pitch, not a transformation promise, not a religion. The app and the guide are tools. What you do with them is yours.

Getting started

1
Create a pod or go solo
Start a pod with up to 7 friends — just add their names. Everything stays on this device. No cost.
2
Follow the weekly rhythm
Each week unlocks one essential. Listen to the audio, read the activity, do the practice, meet with your pod.
3
Check in daily
Mark off each day's tasks. Your pod sees your progress. Your buddy checks in mid-week.

The 8 essentials

  1. Silence — Stillness as a superpower. 3 minutes a day.
  2. Body — Stewardship of the only body you get. Sleep, food, movement.
  3. Skills — Feed, fix, build, grow, finance. Real competence.
  4. Digital — Agency in an extractive attention economy.
  5. Community — People who know your name. The 2am test.
  6. Knowledge — What you still own when the signal dies.
  7. Nature — Sunlight, soil, and slow rhythm.
  8. Convictions — Values that hold under pressure.

Features

🧠 AI Assistant — Ask about the essentials, get challenges, or reflect. Runs locally on your device.

🎙️ Record podcast — In each essential, record a 3-minute audio diary. Stays on your device.

📷 Evidence upload — Add photos or videos to show your progress. Share with your pod via chat apps.

🔒 Privacy mode — Toggle to show a placeholder instead of your evidence. Others see a locked icon.

🔇 Silent partner — Someone in the pod watches your progress silently. Revealed at week 8.

🤝 Buddy pairing — Different partner each week. Check in directly mid-week.

🌙 Dark mode — Profile tab. Toggle inverted colour scheme.

📤 Export / 📥 Import — Profile tab. Migrate data between devices as a .json file.

Service extension

After completing all 8 essentials, unlock the service track: 8 weeks of community service. Complete all 8 and enter the prize draw.

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. No accounts. No cloud storage. No tracking. Share only what you choose, only through your existing chat apps. The AI runs in your browser. Your conversations never leave.
