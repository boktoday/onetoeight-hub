Roots

A trip-hop theme for onetoeight

Trip-hop · 85 BPM E minor Smoky female vocal 3 verses + chorus
Verse 1
[Spoken-breathy, intimate. Beat + sub-bass + vinyl crackle]

We gave them glass to hold
And called it a window to the world
Seven hours a day their eyes go cold
Every notification a new thread pulled

They can reach Tokyo before they learn to breathe
They know every meme but not how to grieve
We gave them everything they could ever need
Except the one thing they can't download

Pre‑Chorus
[String pad swells. Beat builds]

And the silence they avoid
Is the place they'd hear their voice
And the stillness they fill with noise
Is the only real choice

Chorus
[Full beat. Full voice. Wurlitzer and strings]

Give them roots
Not just wings that carry them away
Give them roots
Something real that holds them when they sway
Not a feed, not a screen
Not a filtered in-between
Give them roots
Give them roots

Verse 2
[Beat settles into groove. Warm Wurlitzer piano]

Show them how to sit with what they feel
Without reaching for a screen to steal the space
Let them learn to fix what's real and steel
The nerve to look a stranger in the face

Teach them silence is not emptiness
It's where attention comes back home
Teach them a skill is not a performance
It's dignity you don't perform alone

Chorus

Give them roots
Not just wings that carry them away
Give them roots
Something real that holds them when they sway
Not a feed, not a screen
Not a filtered in-between
Give them roots
Give them roots

Verse 3
[Bass pulse. Strings sustain. Vocals lower, more urgent]

Take them out where the signal dies
Let the dirt get under their nails
Let them learn silence under open skies
The only curriculum that never fails

Show them friendship is not a follower count
Show them a body is not a brand
Show them the cost of every ounce
Of the life they hold in their hand

Chorus

Give them roots
Not just wings that carry them away
Give them roots
Something real that holds them when they sway
Not a brand, not a hype
Not a blue light in the night
Give them roots
Give them roots

Bridge
[Beat drops to a kick and a heartbeat. Stripped back. Just voice and pulse]

What did we actually say
When we handed them the device?
Did we tell them it would eat their days
Or did we call it paradise?

What do they actually know
When the signal finally dies?
Can they make a meal?
Can they hold a conversation?
Can they look you in the eyes?

Final Chorus
[Full arrangement. Strings swell. Vocal belts]

Give them roots! (roots)
Not just wings that carry them away (carry them away)
Give them roots! (roots)
Something real that holds them when they sway
Not a brand, not a hype
Not a blue light in the night
Give them roots!
Give them routes!

Outro
[Everything falls away. A lone piano. A sub-bass note. Vinyl crackle. Fade to silence.]

One to eight
Build a life that holds
One to eight
While the noise unfolds
One to eight
While there's still time

[Silence. 4 beats. Then, a final quiet breath:]

Give them roots.

Production notes

Reference tracks: "Glory Box" (Portishead), "Teardrop" (Massive Attack), "Lebanese Blonde" (Thievery Corporation)

Vocal: Dry, forward in the mix, intimate. The word "roots" in the chorus bends slightly on each repeat — first soft, last almost spoken.

Bridge: Subtle vocal harmony (a third above) on the question lines.

Outro: A single decaying piano note. Then silence.
